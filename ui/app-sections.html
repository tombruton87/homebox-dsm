Sign-ups

Closed: only people invited to a collection can make an account. Open: anyone who can reach Homebox can make one, with a collection of their own — fine for a moment while the family joins, then close them again.

A forgotten password

New password beside someone makes one, shown here once, through Homebox's own password reset: they're signed out on every device and sign in with the new password, then can change it in Homebox (Profile). Homebox never shows anyone's current password, and the package doesn't keep any.
