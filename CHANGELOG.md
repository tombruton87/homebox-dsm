# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-11

First release. Homebox 0.26.2 (the maintained sysadminsmedia fork) in
Container Manager. Your account is made at the first start, with the password
shown once; until it exists, Homebox answers only on the Synology itself, and
sign-ups are closed afterwards.

The DSM window adds:
- **Inventory:** items, what they're worth, locations, attached files and
  warranties for every collection; the warranties ending soon; and a DSM
  notification a week to three months before one ends, once per item.
- **Users:** everyone with an account and their collections, sign-ups open or
  closed, and a new password (shown once) through Homebox's own password reset,
  which signs that person out everywhere.
- **Updates:** Homebox stays at a tested release; the window finds newer ones
  on GitHub and ghcr.io and moves to them after a backup.

Also included: consistent backups with every photo, receipt and manual,
restore and removal.
